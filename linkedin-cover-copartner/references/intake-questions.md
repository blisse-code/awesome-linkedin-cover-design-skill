# Intake question bank

Ask in batches of up to 3. Prefer tappable options with an "Other" path. Skip any question the conversation, memory or uploaded files already answer, and say what you assumed.

## Gate A. Goal and audience

1. What should this banner do first?
   - Get me hired (in-house roles)
   - Win clients or consulting work
   - Attract partners or collaborators
   - Grow mentees, students or community
2. Who must recognise themselves in under a second? (pick up to two)
   - Executives / hiring managers
   - Founders / solopreneurs
   - Peers in my discipline
   - Students / early career
3. After looking, what should they do?
   - Visit my site
   - Open my Featured section
   - Message me
   - Follow me

## Gate B. Substance

4. In one sentence: who do you help, and what changes for them? (free text; offer 3 drafted examples if stuck)
5. Which credentials can you prove if someone asks? Years, launches, clients served, revenue influenced, awards, publications, mentoring hours, certifications. Ask for the exact number and where it is documented. Flag mismatches with resume, headline or site.
6. How do you actually help? (hands-on work, leading teams, advising, teaching, building). This decides whether the copy signals doer, leader, or both.
7. Contact path: URL on the banner, "see Featured" pointer, or both as variants?

## Gate C. Taste

8. Share at least 2 banners, sites, posters or images you like. For each: what do you like about it?
9. Anything you dislike or want to avoid? (stock photos, busy gradients, emoji, neon, corporate blue, etc.)
10. Brand assets: website URL, colour palette, fonts, logo, signature imagery, profile photo.
11. Mode: light, dark, or both to compare?
12. Pattern or texture that represents you (offer concrete options tied to their work, for example dot grid for designers, topographic contours for strategists, circuit or node paths for engineers, film grain for photographers, ledger lines for finance). Ask where it should sit: full bleed and faint, around the hero, or edge only.

## Gate D. Reality

13. Screenshot of your profile on desktop and on mobile (current banner is fine). Used to measure exactly where your photo and UI icons sit.
14. Profile photo: current one, or changing soon? Its dominant colours.

## When answers are thin

- No references: offer 3 contrasting moodboards in words (Bold Authority, Textured Minimalist, Portfolio Showcase) and ask them to pick or mix.
- No proof: build without a credential line and say so; never invent one.
- "Just do it": proceed with stated assumptions, deliver max 2 options, and ask the 3 most decision-changing questions alongside.
